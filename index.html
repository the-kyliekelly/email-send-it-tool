Not sure which to pick? Think about the last conversation you had with a client or someone in your community. What did they say? What did they need? That's probably your angle.
Step 3 – write your email ~ 10–15 min
Open your email platform and write using the structure below – one section at a time. Aim for 150–250 words total. Toggle the example below if you need a reference.
A few things to remember: don't open with "I hope this finds you well." Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences. Sign off like a human. One idea, one email – resist the urge to add more.
Your subject line
0 / 50
Aim for under 50 characters – short subject lines get more opens on mobile.
💡
Your subject line is your open rate. One line, one promise – if you're tempted to use a slash or "and", that's two emails. The best subject lines are specific, a little curious, and sound like something a real person wrote.
